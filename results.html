Results

One engagement, documented honestly

A recent 6-month Local SEO engagement for a local business. Below: what was done, the measured outcomes, the original screenshots — and the limitations you should know about.

01 — The work

What the six months contained

Starting point: a local business barely visible in local search and Maps, with an unoptimized Business Profile, technical issues, and thin content — while competitors captured nearly all local demand.

The program: full Local SEO and Business Profile optimization, on-page improvements, technical fixes (speed, crawling, indexing), keyword-mapped local content, citations, and continuous tracking — no paid ads involved.

  • Local SEO + Business Profile overhaul for map and local-pack visibility.
  • Technical and on-page fixes so Google could crawl, understand, and trust the site.
  • Content + authority building matched to real customer intent and measured monthly.

The business-meaningful movement is in users and key events: roughly 1,100 conversions or important actions over six months.

03 — Limitations

What these numbers don’t prove

Honest marketing requires the other half of the story:

  • One engagement, one market. Your competition, starting point, and budget differ — your trajectory will too.
  • Traffic isn’t revenue. These are visibility and engagement metrics. What matters for you is qualified enquiries — which is what monthly reporting tracks.
  • No before/after baseline shown. These are engagement totals, not lift-vs-baseline claims. Your own audit will establish your baseline first.

If a marketer shows you numbers without any of this context, ask for it — from me or anyone else.
