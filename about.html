About

The specialist behind the rankings

One person, one craft: making local businesses findable. Here is who I am, how I work, and what I won’t do.

From curious kid in Netrakona to SEO specialist

I’m Arif Mia, based in Barhatta, Netrakona, Bangladesh. My work started with a simple question: why do some businesses show up on Google while better ones stay invisible? Years of hands-on projects — auditing sites, fixing profiles, studying competitors — turned that curiosity into a profession.

Today I help local businesses improve their visibility across Google Search, Google Maps, and AI-powered search. My method is deliberately boring in the best way: understand the business, fix the foundations, target real customer intent, build authority, and measure everything.

I don’t chase ranking spikes. I build visibility that survives algorithm updates and compounds into calls, bookings, and revenue.

01 — Working principles

What you can hold me to

  • You talk to me, always. Strategy, execution, and reporting are mine personally — never silently outsourced.
  • White-hat only. No link schemes, no fake reviews, no cloaking — nothing that risks your business for a quick spike.
  • Plain-English reporting. You’ll see what was done, what changed, and what’s next — without jargon or vanity charts.
  • No guaranteed positions. Nobody can promise #1. I promise expert process and honest measurement instead.
  • No work for shady businesses. Nothing illegal, unethical, or against Google’s guidelines — I check before we start.
  • Revenue over rankings. Every recommendation must connect to visibility, enquiries, or customers — or it doesn’t ship.

03 — The path here

How the craft was built

  1. Curiosity

    In Barhatta, Netrakona: why do some businesses appear on Google and others don’t? Self-teaching begins — sites, search behavior, experiments.

  2. Practice

    Hands-on projects: optimizing websites and Business Profiles, analyzing competitors, learning what actually moves rankings and revenue.

  3. Profession

    Proven systems become client work: Local SEO, Maps visibility, technical fixes, and content strategies with measured outcomes — 10+ businesses and counting.

  4. AI search

    Now extending the craft to answer engines: AEO and GEO so clients stay visible in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Gemini.
