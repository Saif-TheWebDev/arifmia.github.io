No — and you should distrust anyone who does. Rankings depend on factors nobody controls outright. I commit to expert work, honest reporting, and steady improvement in visibility, traffic quality, and enquiries.

Yes. Every call, audit, and change is handled by me personally. If I’m ever unavailable, I’ll tell you rather than quietly handing you off.
